# GO Stores / GO Garage: public customer evidence audit

Research date: 2026-11-16. Public web search and page reads only; no login, account access, or access-control bypass. This is a bounded evidence sample, not a representative sentiment study.

## Finding that can safely be used

No directly attributable, dated customer review or social-media comment was successfully inspected in this sample. It is therefore **not supported** to state that GO Stores has recurring bad workmanship, slow responses, unfair prices, rejected warranties, or dissatisfied customers. No reliable customer-response rate, sentiment percentage, or competitor satisfaction comparison can be calculated.

The strongest observable weakness is **inconsistent third-party business information**, subject to verifying the current official branches. Listings differ over street number (9 versus 10 Saqr Qoraish), contact numbers, and branding. This can create discovery and branch-selection friction; the friction is an inference, not an observed customer complaint. The GO Garage Nozha listing may describe another branch, an old location, or a different entity; affiliation remains unconfirmed.

## Recommended evidence to obtain next

1. Confirm current official branches and their exact public Google Business Profile URLs with the owner, then inspect review dates and responses without conflating branches.
2. Inspect publicly accessible social threads in a normal authorized browser if available, preserving direct post/comment URLs and separating promotional captions from customer testimony.
3. For a small review sample, record date, branch, service, first-person allegation, response and resolution; count only unique experiences and never infer a recurring pattern from one post.
4. Request approved price/package and written warranty documents before proposing customer-facing policy language. Repository seed warranty terms are examples, not evidence of business policy.

No personal vulnerabilities, private records, customer names or contact details were collected into this report.
