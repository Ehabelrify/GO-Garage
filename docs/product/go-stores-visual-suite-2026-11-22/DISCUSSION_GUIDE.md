# Maher discussion guide

## Meeting outcome

Use the visual suite to confirm how GO Stores should operate, identify the decisions that affect implementation, and separate business approval from design preference. Do not activate settings or describe a board as already live merely because the direction is accepted.

Suggested duration: 75-90 minutes.

| Time | Boards | Discussion | Decision to capture |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 5 min | 01 | Brand recognition and product tone | Master logo, red/gold hierarchy, permitted premium treatment |
| 10 min | 02, 09 | Owner priorities and rollout | Top exceptions, report cadence, pilot branch(es), readiness owner |
| 15 min | 03 | Reception and WhatsApp | Six-number ownership, central team, service windows, handoff, booking authority |
| 10 min | 04 | Workshop | Real stages, assignment rules, intake evidence, QC authority, shared devices |
| 10 min | 05 | Customer history | Required customer/vehicle fields, privacy, archive authority, special-request approval |
| 15 min | 06 | Commercial flow | Catalogue owner, quotation approval, deposits, reservation validity, promotion rules |
| 10 min | 07 | Warranty and aftercare | Fixed/lifetime meaning, issued terms, claims authority, public QR fields |
| 10 min | 08 | Finance and future stock | Metric definitions, source records, accountant input, inventory priority |

## Board-by-board prompts

### Brand system

- Which existing GO Stores logo file is authoritative for digital use?
- Is the crown limited to premium campaigns?
- May yellow-gold identify premium packages and attention states, or only marketing?
- Who approves Arabic and English public-facing copy?

### Owner overview

- What are the five questions Maher asks staff most often during the day?
- Which numbers are useful daily, weekly, and monthly?
- Which values are currently available from reliable records?
- What should trigger an immediate alert rather than appear in a digest?

### Reception and central inbox

- Confirm that all six numbers feed one handling team and that the receiving number remains the reply channel.
- Confirm whether selected branch, not receiving number, determines availability.
- Define service hours, escalation expectations, reassignment authority, and after-hours behavior.
- Confirm which bookings can be automatically confirmed after customer confirmation and capacity recheck.
- Identify the person responsible for Meta setup, templates, credentials, and number cutover.

### Booking and transport

- Confirm branch calendars, bay types/counts, daily drop-off limits, service duration, and override authority.
- Confirm which vehicle details are mandatory before a booking.
- Define collection, return, tow, chauffeur/VIP responsibility, availability, charges, and exceptions.

### Workshop and QC

- Confirm the real stage vocabulary in Arabic and English.
- Define when a booking becomes a job and when a technician may be assigned.
- Confirm intake fields and required photo angles.
- Identify who may pass/fail QC, create rework, move a job backwards, or reopen delivered work.
- Confirm shared-tablet versus personal-device use and inactivity/security expectations.

### Customer and Vehicle 360

- Confirm the minimum customer identity, consent, and vehicle fields.
- Decide who may edit, archive, restore, merge, or export records.
- Confirm how duplicate phones/plates and ownership changes should be handled.
- Define the approval path and response promise for special requests.

### Catalogue, quotes, promotions, reservations, and benefits

- Obtain the approved package/price sheet and identify its owner.
- Confirm package dimensions: vehicle category/model, film grade/thickness, roof, add-ons, labor/tax, branch availability, warranty reference.
- Define quote validity, revision approval, customer-agreement evidence, and who may override price.
- Define deposits, refund/cancellation disposition, expiry, and restoration for later-installation reservations.
- Define warranty-card, membership, referral, and multi-car qualification and duplicate-redemption rules.
- Define included benefits, limits, eligibility, responsible team, and fulfillment evidence.

### Warranty and aftercare

- Obtain a blank current warranty certificate and approved terms.
- Define fixed-term and lifetime coverage without converting lifetime into an arbitrary month count.
- Confirm exclusions, inspection/maintenance requirements, claim authority, remedies, and transfer rules.
- Approve the exact public QR fields and whether verification is enabled.
- Confirm invoice currencies, tax treatment, numbering, payment capture, and printing requirements.

### Finance, reporting, and inventory

- Have Maher/accountant approve definitions for quote value, collection, outstanding balance, recognized revenue, direct cost, overhead, refund, and gross profit.
- Identify the source of each value and correction/reversal rules.
- Decide whether inventory is needed for the pilot or a later release.
- If included, define branch ownership, unit, reorder level, adjustment authority, purchasing flow, and low-stock alerts.

### Access, readiness, and rollout

- Confirm named staff, verified work email, role, branches, and capability exceptions.
- Confirm pilot branch(es), staff participants, training owner, fallback process, and acceptance authority.
- Assign migration, dummy-data purge, backup/restore, provider, privacy, and support owners.

## Decision record

For every decision record:

- Topic and decision.
- Approver and date.
- Business-wide or branch-specific scope.
- Effective date and exceptions.
- Evidence supplied, such as policy sheet, price list, warranty document, accountant definition, or provider confirmation.
- Product configuration or implementation consequence.
- Follow-up owner and due date.

Leave the item **Unresolved** when evidence or an accountable approver is missing.
