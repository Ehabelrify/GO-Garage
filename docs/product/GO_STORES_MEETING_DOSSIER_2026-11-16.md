## What to know before walking in

**The strongest meeting angle is helping Go Stores preserve and deliver its customer promises.** The business advertises several film grades, changing packages, long warranties, deferred installation, referral discounts, and transport/aftercare benefits. Customer conversations ask about model-specific prices, turnaround, delivery, payment and accessory fitment. ## 5. Warranty: strong sales promise, terms still to confirm

The evidence establishes advertised durations and a warranty-card promotion. It does **not** establish a complete contract, a successful claim, or a failure to honor coverage. No complete blank certificate, exclusions or formal claim/transfer policy was inspected.

Resolve these before configuring customer-facing answers:

- What does lifetime mean: purchaser, vehicle, ownership period, film life, or another defined period?
- Which entity issues coverage, and what differs between film/manufacturer coverage and installation workmanship?
- What is covered for yellowing, lifting, bubbles, delamination, fading, repainting, removal damage, accidents or stone impact? What remedy, labor charge or limit applies?
- Are factory paint, repainted panels, replacement panels and subsequent repairs treated differently?
- Is maintenance required for validity? What are the frequency and eligibility rules for free maintenance/washing?
- Does coverage follow the vehicle at resale? How are card loss, transferred ownership and prior repairs verified?
- Who approves claims and exceptions? What photos, invoice, product/batch, install date and maintenance evidence are required?

The owner challenges competitors' six-month maintenance language in a **12 March 2025** post, while a **5 November 2026** promotion offers free maintenance and washes throughout warranty. These can refer to different things; they are not enough to declare a contradiction or invent a maintenance condition. Ask what each means operationally. [Maintenance commentary](https://www.facebook.com/groups/5125742449/permalink/10163308131997450/), [aftercare offer](https://www.facebook.com/groups/5125742449/permalink/10166131659747450/).

## 8. A focused meeting and demonstration

**Recommended demonstration:** take a fictional customer who saw one dated promotion, contacts one reception number, chooses a branch, receives a scope-specific quote and feasible date, and later asks about warranty. Show where the agreed package, customer acceptance, intake evidence, job status and warranty terms would be retrieved. Use explicitly labeled sample data; do not imply a public advertisement is already approved policy.

Ask these questions in order, adapting the existing [owner meeting playbook](OWNER_MEETING_PLAYBOOK.md):

1. “Who is attending, and who approves operations, prices, warranty exceptions, marketing offers and software spending?” Confirm current roles before relying on profile titles.
2. “Which branches and services are active today, and which business numbers does the central team handle?”
3. “Which of these published packages is still available? What changes for SUV size, roof, film finish, add-ons and tax?”
4. “When an offer is reserved today and installed months later, what locks the price, and what are the deposit, cancellation and expiry rules?”
5. “What must reception check before saying same day, and how is transport or VIP service reserved?”
6. “Can we follow one recent enquiry through quote, deposit, booking, installation, QC and collection using redacted examples?”
7. “Can we inspect a blank approved warranty certificate and a redacted claim? What exactly is lifetime?”
8. “How are free washes, maintenance, referral discounts and multi-car offers tracked and limited?”
9. “Which questions may automation answer, and which must be assigned to a person?”
10. “Which daily exception most needs your attention, and what baseline would make a pilot's value measurable?”

Existing session/project context says **six WhatsApp numbers handled by one central team**, with the requested branch controlling availability. That is user-provided project context, not a count proven by the social sources. The checked-out app has customer/vehicle records, booking/capacity checks, jobs, warranties/claims and invoice drafts. WhatsApp remains a development simulator with provider delivery disabled; inventory, full accounting/POS and live/scheduled messaging are not implemented. Do not demonstrate future capabilities as live. [Project instructions](../../AGENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md), [workflow context](WORKFLOW_PRODUCT_RESEARCH_2026-11-16.md).

Request a small evidence pack after discovery: current price/service matrix, blank warranty terms, active branch/hours/number map, promotion/deposit rules, role/approval map, and redacted examples of one booking, one handover and one claim. No passwords, customer database dump or account-session export is needed.
