# Review C: owner, 360 px phone, EN + AR

Scope: 13 EN pages and 14 AR pages (AR also has `bookings` and a 144,396 px `quotes`; EN lacks both). Every page was viewed via slices. Tall pages were viewed at the first slice or two only, so deep-page findings are inferred from the repeated card pattern.

## Verdict
The phone layout is a desktop app squeezed into 360 px, and it is not ready. Three problems matter most. (1) Money KPIs overflow their tiles on Overview. (2) Several pages are cut off horizontally. (3) Pages are absurdly long, with no paging: 144k px (AR quotes), 91k (warranties), 36k (overview). The mobile bottom bar is solid, but the persistent top chrome eats about 155 px, and there is no "More" sheet in any shot, so it is unreviewed. The card pattern is consistent and readable. Plates and Arabic names mix directions inside one row, which looks messy.

Source files (verified to exist): `apps/web/src/features/overview/overview.css`, `shared/styles/components.css` (`.data-card`, lines 49-59), `shared/styles/layout.css` (mobile nav at line 64, `.main-area` at line 127), `shared/components/DataTable.tsx`, `TabBar.tsx`, `FilterBar.tsx`, `features/finance/FinancePage.tsx`, `features/bookings/BookingsAndTransport.tsx`.

## What would make this noticeably better
1. A dedicated mobile Overview: 2x2 KPI grid with compact money values, then a short prioritised "Needs attention" list.
2. Paging or infinite scroll with a sticky filter and result count on every list.
3. A single sticky, slim header (about 52 px) instead of 3 rows of controls.
4. One plate component, one money component, one date formatter, used everywhere, bidi-isolated.
5. A mobile type scale and a 44px touch-target rule in tokens, with a lint or visual check for horizontal overflow at 360 px.
6. Localise seed or content data in AR (benefit names, "Fourth car") or label it as user-entered.
7. Add a sheet or select for tabs and a bottom-sheet filter drawer instead of a stack of selects.
